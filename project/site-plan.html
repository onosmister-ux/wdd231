Nigerian Food Price Tracker - Site Plan

Site Name

Nigerian Food Price Tracker

This name was selected because it clearly describes the purpose of the website. The site helps users track and compare food prices across different Nigerian markets and locations.

Optional Domain: naijafoodprices.ng

Site Purpose

The Nigerian Food Price Tracker website provides information about food prices in Nigeria. Users can browse food items, compare prices, monitor price trends, and submit price updates from their local markets. The website aims to help families, students, traders, and consumers make informed purchasing decisions.

Planned Pages

  • Home Page – Featured food prices, market highlights, and search functionality.
  • Food Prices Page – Displays food items, prices, locations, and comparisons.
  • Submit Prices Page – Allows users to submit updated food prices through a form.

Scenarios

  • What is the current price of rice, beans, and garri in different Nigerian markets?
  • Which market currently offers the lowest price for a specific food item?
  • Have food prices increased or decreased compared to previous weeks?
  • How can I submit updated food prices from my local market?
